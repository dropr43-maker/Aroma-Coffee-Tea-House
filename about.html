Our Story

Aroma Coffee & Tea House was established in 2020 because of a simple love for good coffee and tea. Our founders saw that many people could not find quality, fairly priced drinks in one friendly place, so they opened a small shop to share the best of local coffee and tea with their community.

Our Mission

Our mission is to serve high-quality coffee and tea that is carefully sourced, freshly prepared and fairly priced, while giving every customer a warm and welcoming experience.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted and well-loved coffee and tea brand, known for quality, great taste and support for the farmers who grow our products.
