Our Programmes

Pick a path that fits where you want to go in tech.

Each programme is designed to take you from the basics to real, job-ready skills. You will learn from experienced lecturers, work on practical projects and build a portfolio that shows what you can do. Whether you love building things, working with numbers or keeping systems running, there is a place for you here.

Software Development

Learn to design, write and test programs and web applications.

You will start with the fundamentals of programming and slowly move into building full websites and apps. Along the way, you will learn how to plan a project, work with a team, fix bugs and keep your code clean and reliable. By the end, you will have real projects to show employers and the confidence to keep learning on your own.

Data Analysis

Learn to collect, clean and understand data so it can guide smart decisions.

Data is only useful when someone can make sense of it, and this programme teaches you how. You will work with real data sets, learn to spot patterns and present your findings in a way that is clear and easy to understand. These skills are in demand in almost every industry, from business and health to education and government.

Networking

Learn how computers connect, how networks are set up and how to keep them running securely.

Every school, business and hospital depends on strong networks, and this programme prepares you to build and look after them. You will practise setting up devices, fixing connection problems and protecting systems from threats. The hands-on lab work gives you the experience you need to start a career in network support and security.

AI Technology

Learn how artificial intelligence works and how it is used to solve real problems.

This programme introduces you to the ideas behind machine learning, chatbots and smart automation. You will learn how AI systems are trained, how to use them responsibly and how to build simple projects that put them to work. As more industries adopt AI, these skills will open doors to exciting new careers and help you stay ahead in a fast-changing world.
